======================================================================
 CASTALIA 92  -  FACT SHEET
======================================================================

PRODUCT
    Name ............... Castalia 92
    What it is ......... A graphical operating environment (desktop
                         shell) for DOS-compatible 386 systems
    Version at press ... 0.56  (the project ships small, frequent
                         releases on its way to 1.0)
    Price .............. Free
    License ............ MIT (source included)
    Distribution ....... source + a ready-to-run release folder, at
      https://github.com/Tombatossals-Softworks-LLC/CastaliaOS-92

DEVELOPER
    Studio ............. Tombatossals Softworks LLC
    Created by ......... Dave Abellan
    Contact ............ hello@tombatossalssoftworks.com
    Web ................ https://www.tombatossalssoftworks.com

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
    CPU ................ Intel 386SX or better (486 recommended,
                         Pentium feels like cheating)
    RAM ................ 4 MB (the shell keeps ~120 KB resident when
                         launching DOS programs; a free-memory mode
                         unloads it entirely for big games)
    Video .............. VGA: 320x200x256 (the flagship mode) or
                         640x480x16
    Input .............. Keyboard; Microsoft-compatible mouse
                         recommended
    Audio (optional) ... PC speaker (always works), Sound Blaster
                         for digital WAV, AdLib/OPL2 FM synth for
                         polyphonic MIDI, Covox Speech Thing and
                         Disney Sound Source on the printer port
    OS ................. DOS 3.3 or later (real mode, no extenders)

THE NUMBERS
    Built-in programs .. 30+ (file manager, editor, paint, media
                         player, system inspector, benchmark...)
    Games .............. 13 (minesweeper, solitaire, snake, falling
                         blocks, sokoban, reversi, 2048 and friends)
    Colour themes ...... 18, all live-previewable
    Screensaver ........ 16 demoscene effects (plasma, voxel terrain,
                         textured raycaster, kaleidoscope...)
    Wallpapers ......... 8 original GIF wallpapers + tiling papers
    Source ............. 65 C files, ~33,000 lines of C89
    External deps ...... none.  Not one library.

ONE-SENTENCE DESCRIPTIONS (PICK YOUR FLAVOUR)
    Neutral:  Castalia 92 is a free, open-source graphical desktop
              for DOS that gives 386-class hardware overlapping
              windows, a taskbar, media playback and thirty built-in
              programs.
    Fun:      Castalia 92 is the Windows 95 your 386 was promised
              and never got - three years early and 120 KB small.
    Nerd:     A single-binary C89 desktop shell for real-mode DOS
              with a double-buffered Mode 13h compositor, fixed-point
              demoscene screensaver and OPL2 General-MIDI playback.
