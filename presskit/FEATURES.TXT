======================================================================
 CASTALIA 92  -  THE GUIDED TOUR
======================================================================
(Everything below ships in the box.  Nothing is a mock-up; the shots
in IMAGES\ are rendered from the shell's own drawing code.)

THE DESKTOP
    * Overlapping windows: drag, resize, minimize, maximize, shade
      (roll-up), cascade.  Double-click a title bar to maximize, just
      where your muscle memory expects it.
    * A taskbar with one button per window, a tray speaker icon, a
      live clock - and the Start button ("Inicio"; the studio is
      Spanish and so is the castle on the crest).
    * A cascading Start menu with flyout submenus and per-item icons,
      real message boxes, caption-button glyphs, a first-run "Click
      here to begin" balloon and a proper shutdown sequence.
    * Desktop icons you can drag between grid slots, backed by real
      .ICO loading: genuine Windows 95 icon files render mapped to
      the theme palette.  Or draw your own in the built-in editor.
    * Wallpapers: eight original 320x200 GIF pictures (a castle, a
      reef, dunes, a night sky...) plus tiling papers, or a plain,
      dignified teal.

THE PROGRAMS (a selection of the 30+)
    * Disk Cabinet - the file manager: browse, launch, copy, rename,
      delete, with drive bar and scrollable listing.
    * Scribe's Desk - the text editor, with find and word wrap.
    * Sketch Pad - paint; doubles as the icon editor (save as .ICN,
      point a desktop icon at it, done).
    * Gramophone - the media player: WAV as real digital audio on a
      Sound Blaster, a Covox Speech Thing or a Disney Sound Source on
      the printer port (or pulse-width tricks on the bare PC speaker),
      polyphonic MIDI with General-MIDI voices on an AdLib/OPL2 FM
      chip.  LCD strip with a crawling title, spectrum analyzer with
      gravity and peak caps, folder playlists, seek bar, repeat.
    * System Inspector - a live animated dashboard: hardware readout
      with status LEDs, an oscilloscope, an LED clock, charging
      memory/disk gauges and a BIOS equipment strip.
    * Benchmark - a six-test suite (integer, memory, video kernels)
      indexed against a 386SX/16, with a composite CASTALIA SCORE
      and a machine-class verdict, drawn as animated bars.
    * Plus: calculator, calendar, agenda (persistent to-do list),
      hex viewer, character map, Mandelbrot/Julia explorer with
      click-to-zoom, stopwatch, Colors board, Find File, Picture
      Show (GIF viewer), Cinema (FLI/FLC animation player), Eyes
      (they follow the pointer; they blink; they judge).

THE GAMES (all thirteen, all with sound effects and high scores)
    Minefield (minesweeper), Patience (klondike with the winning
    cascade), Reversi, Serpent (snake), Breaker (breakout), Quadrix
    (falling blocks), Depot (sokoban, ten verified floors), Echo
    (sound-memory), Lights Out, Fifteen Puzzle, Tic Tac Toe, Pong
    and 2048.

THE THEMES
    Eighteen complete colour schemes, each restyling every pixel of
    chrome: classic, penumbra, bureau, winsteel, moncloa, workbench
    (an Amiga homage), ocean, rose, midnight, amber, matrix, redmond,
    sunset, forest, hotdog (loud, period, beloved), slate, sakura and
    dos.  The Settings panel shows every theme as a window-mockup
    swatch, and pointing at one paints a live miniature screen in its
    colours before you commit.  Themes cross-fade when applied, and a
    [colors] section in the INI lets you override any single slot.

THE SCREENSAVER
    After a configurable idle delay the desktop dissolves into a reel
    of sixteen demoscene effects, cross-fading one to the next:
    plasma, copper bars, warp starfield, fire, the Boing ball, a
    rotozoomer, a tunnel, matrix rain, tumbling vector balls, a giant
    sine scroller, a twister column, fireworks, a rainbow wire-frame
    cube, a Comanche-style voxel terrain flyover, a Wolfenstein-style
    textured raycast corridor, and a DAC-cycled kaleidoscope.  All of
    it is fixed-point integer mathematics on the shared sine table -
    no FPU, no lookup cheats beyond what 1992 would bless.

THE PLUMBING (the parts power users ask about)
    * CASTALIA.INI: theme, video mode, wallpaper, screensaver delay,
      sound, launcher shortcuts, per-slot colour overrides - all in
      one commented file, all documented in docs/FORMATS.TXT.
    * Free-memory launch: mark a shortcut freemem=true and Castalia
      unloads itself completely to give a big DOS game every last
      kilobyte, then returns when the game exits.
    * An installer (INSTALL.EXE) with progress bar and PATH option.
    * Persistent high scores, agenda and settings survive reboots.
