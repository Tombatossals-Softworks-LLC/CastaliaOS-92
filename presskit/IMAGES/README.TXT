======================================================================
 CASTALIA 92  -  PRESS IMAGES
======================================================================

All images are rendered at the shell's native 320x200 resolution by
Castalia 92's own drawing routines (via the project's host-side
verification pipeline), then upscaled 2x with hard pixels to 640x400
for print/web.  They may be reproduced freely in coverage of the
project.  Higher resolutions or specific scenes on request:
hello@tombatossalssoftworks.com

    LOGO.PNG ....... The castle crest (the actual 13x10-pixel Start
                     button art, enlarged) with the wordmark.  White
                     background, suitable for headers.

    DESKTOP.PNG .... The flagship shot: the Gramophone playing a
                     MIDI on the classic teal desktop - LCD strip,
                     spectrum analyzer with peak caps, folder
                     playlist, transport row, seek bar - with desktop
                     icons, taskbar, tray and clock.

    SETTINGS.PNG ... The Settings panel's Theme tab: all eighteen
                     colour schemes as window-mockup swatches, with
                     the live preview strip showing "sakura" under
                     the pointer.

    KALEIDO.PNG .... Screensaver effect 16, KALEIDOSCOPE: the plasma
                     field folded into eight-fold symmetry, animated
                     by palette rotation.  (A still frame; on the
                     machine it flows continuously.)

    CORRIDOR.PNG ... Screensaver effect 15, CORRIDOR: a Wolfenstein-
                     style textured raycast maze with distance fog,
                     walked by an auto-pilot.

    VOXEL.PNG ...... Screensaver effect 14, VOXEL LANDSCAPE: a
                     Comanche-style terrain flyover, ray-marched in
                     Q8 fixed-point - grass, lakes, beaches, snow.

    WIREFRAM.PNG ... Screensaver effect 13, WIREFRAME: the rainbow
                     wire cube tumbling on three axes with depth-
                     shaded corners.  (8.3 filename, of course.)
